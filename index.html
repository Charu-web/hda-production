Our Heritage & Vision

ABOUT HDA PRODUCTION

ARCHITECTING FASHION EXCELLENCE

HDA Production was founded in Mumbai with a clear vision: to set global benchmarks in luxury fashion show production, model management, and celebrity campaign choreography.

Over the past decade, our production house has orchestrated over 500 fashion week showcases, bridal galas, and luxury brand launches across India, Europe, and the Middle East.

Our core pillars: Uncompromising Elegance, Technical Precision, World-Class Talent Management, and Immersive Storytelling.
